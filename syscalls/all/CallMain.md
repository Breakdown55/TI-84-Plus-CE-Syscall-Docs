### Address: $020188

This routine calls the current context's [Main](../../memory/all/cxMain.md) vector.

### Inputs:
* [`cxMain`](../../memory/all/cxMain.md): Context's current Main vector.

### Outputs:
* Calls the vector.

### Destroys:
* Depends on the vector.
